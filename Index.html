    Full reference and preferences

    Use Ctrl on Windows/Linux or Cmd on macOS. Single-key navigation shortcuts work outside typing fields when no dialog is open; they pause during text composition.

    Sheet is your editable issue register. Change cells directly, or open an issue for every detail.

    Kanban keeps work moving. Drag a card to change status or order. Use the card's Move button for status and legal Epic moves with keyboard or touch.

    Gantt shows your schedule. Drag a bar to move its dates; drag its right edge to change its due date. Open an issue for precise dates and dependencies.

    Hierarchy: epics contain stories; tasks and bugs can belong to either. Choose an epic under Epics & connection to focus on its descendants.

    CSV export includes all fields for the currently filtered issues.

    Move issue

    Moves to the end of the destination column. Other issues keep their order.
